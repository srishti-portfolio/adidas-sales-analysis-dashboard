# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses Adidas sales performance across the United States for 2020 and 2021. It tracks key business KPIs and breaks sales down by month, state, region, product and retailer, so stakeholders can spot trends and make data-driven decisions.

## 📌 Problem Statement

The business needs an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall sales, profit, units sold, average selling price and profit margin.
- Analyse monthly sales trends to identify seasonal patterns.
- Compare sales performance across regions and states.
- Evaluate the contribution of different retailers and product categories.
- Filter the data by sales method, month and year.
- Identify top-performing products and retailers to support strategic planning.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | Overall revenue generated | **$899.90M** |
| **Total Profit** | Total profit earned from sales | **$332.13M** |
| **Total Units Sold** | Number of units sold | **18M** |
| **Average Price per Unit** | Average selling price of products | **$45.22** |
| **Profit Margin** | Percentage profit earned on sales | **42.30%** |

*Values shown are for the full dataset with no filters applied.*

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

| Visual | Chart Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Sales Trend | Area chart | Shows sales performance across months and seasonal patterns |
| State Wise Sales | Filled map | Shows the geographical distribution of sales across US states |
| Region Wise Sales | Donut chart | Compares each region's share of revenue |
| Product Wise Sales | Bar chart | Identifies the best-selling product categories |
| Retailer Wise Sales | Bar chart | Compares retailer performance by total sales |

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)

- **Sales Method:** In-store, Online, Outlet
- **Year:** 2020, 2021
- **Month:** Jan to Dec
- **Reset button:** Clears all selected filters in one click

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Seasonality:** Sales peak in **July ($95M)** and **August ($92M)** and are lowest in **March ($57M)**. There is another rise in **December ($86M)**, likely driven by holiday demand.
- **Top region:** The **West** region contributes the largest share of sales (**~30%**), followed by the **Northeast (~21%)**.
- **Top states:** California, New York, Texas and Florida show the highest sales on the map.
- **Top product:** **Men's Street Footwear** is the best-selling category (**$209M**), followed by **Women's Apparel ($179M)**.
- **Top retailer:** **West Gear** leads with **$243M** in sales, followed by **Foot Locker ($220M)** and **Sports Direct ($182M)**.
- **Profitability:** An overall profit margin of **42.30%** shows strong profitability across channels.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop:** data modelling, DAX measures and visualisation
- **Power Query:** data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX:** KPI calculations (Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin and others)

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The dashboard gives a quick, interactive overview of Adidas sales performance. It shows that summer months, the West region, Men's Street Footwear and West Gear are the biggest drivers of revenue, which can help guide inventory planning, marketing campaigns and retailer partnerships.
